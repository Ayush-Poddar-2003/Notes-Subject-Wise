# Risk
- Possibility of an uncertain event that may negatively affect a software project.
- It impacts time, cost, scope, or quality.
- Every risk has:
  - **Probability** (chance of occurring)
  - **Impact** (damage if it occurs)
- Risks cannot be eliminated but they can be minimized and controlled.


## Categories of Risk

## Technical Risk
- Use of new or untested technology
- Integration issues
- Performance problems
- Technical complexity
- Lack of technical knowledge

## Project Management Risk
- Wrong time or cost estimation
- Poor project planning
- Ineffective communication
- Scope creep (extra features added without control)
- Improper resource allocation

## Organizational Risk
- Lack of management support
- High employee turnover
- Changes in organizational structure
- Insufficient training
- Internal conflicts

## External Risk
- Vendor delays
- Market changes
- Government rules or legal changes
- Natural disasters
- Political or economic instability

## Business Risk
- Product may fail in the market
- Customer needs may change
- Competitors may release better products
- Budget cuts or financial issues

# Risk Identification
- Process of finding and listing all possible risks in the project.
- Output: **Risk Register**

## Risk Identification Techniques
- Brainstorming
- Expert interviews
- SWOT analysis
- Checklists
- Reviewing past projects
- Requirement analysis
- Studying project documents

# <CENTER>Risk Assessment
- Evaluating risks based on seriousness.
- Helps decide which risks need priority action.

## Qualitative Assessment
- Probability: High / Medium / Low
- Impact: High / Medium / Low
- Results shown in a **Risk Matrix**


## Quantitative Assessment
- Numerical probability values
- Numerical impact values (cost/time)
- Expected Monetary Value (EMV)
- Sensitivity analysis


# <CENTER>Risk Mitigation
- Steps taken to reduce probability or impact of risks.

### Strategies for Mitigation
- Avoidance: Change plan to avoid the risk.
- Reduction: Take actions to reduce risk.
- Transfer: Outsource or shift responsibility (insurance, vendor).
- Acceptance: Accept if impact is low.

# Risk Monitoring
- Continuous tracking of identified risks.
- Checking if risk probability or impact is increasing.
- Updating the risk register.

# Risk Management
- Entire process of identifying, assessing, mitigating, and monitoring risks.
- Ensures project stays on schedule, on budget, and on quality.
